# Code of Conduct

Short version: behave like an adult.

Vega is a technical project. What happens here is about code, protocol and product. Keep it there and you will never need to think about this document again.

**Expected**

- Argue about the work, not the person.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith until they show otherwise.
- Disagreement is useful. Contempt is not.
- If you turn out to be wrong, say so and carry on. Nobody is keeping score.

**Not acceptable**

- Harassment, threats, or following someone around the project to attack them.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information.

That is the entire list, and it is deliberately not exhaustive. The standard is adult, respectful behaviour; the maintainer decides where that line falls in practice.

Enforcement is unglamorous: you may be asked to stop, and if it continues, you may be removed. There is no committee, no process to litigate, and no appeal beyond a reasonable conversation.

Vega is for anyone who wants to use Nostr. Who you are, where you come from and what you believe are not this project's business, and will not be used to include or exclude you here. The work is the only thing being judged.

Be well.
